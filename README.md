# ✈️ AVIATION AI COMMUNICATION
### AI-Assisted Passenger Communication for Flight Disruptions

**Project Overview | Prompt Engineering | Source Verification | Human Review**

---

## 🛫 1. Project Overview

**Aviation AI Communication** is an AI-assisted passenger communication workflow designed to improve the accuracy, clarity, and consistency of airline communications during flight disruptions.

The project combines structured prompt engineering, source-based fact-checking, multilingual consistency verification, and human review to produce reliable passenger messages across multiple communication channels.

Using fictional flight scenarios, the project demonstrates how AI tools can support airline service teams while maintaining human accountability and operational accuracy.

## 🎯 2. Business Problem

Airline service teams must communicate changing flight information quickly and consistently across different channels. Incorrect timings, unsupported benefit claims, unclear updates, and inconsistent messages can lead to passenger confusion and reduced trust.

This project addresses these challenges through a structured workflow that helps ensure communications are:

- ✅ Factually accurate and source-verified
- 📢 Clear, concise, and passenger-friendly
- 🔄 Consistent when operational timings change
- 🌐 Aligned across English and Hindi communications
- 👥 Reviewed by a human before final use

**Project objective:** To develop a repeatable AI-assisted process for drafting, verifying, correcting, and updating passenger communications during flight disruptions.

## 🚀 3. Key Features

### 💬 Multi-Channel Passenger Communication
Creation of passenger emails, airport announcements, SMS notifications, and service desk responses tailored to different audiences and communication channels.

### 🧠 Prompt Engineering
Comparison of six prompting strategies to evaluate their impact on accuracy, completeness, clarity, tone, and usefulness.

### 🔎 Source-Based Verification
Systematic checking of generated content against supplied flight details and fictional airline policies to identify unsupported claims and factual errors.

### 🔄 Dynamic Update Management
Revision of passenger communications when expected departure timings change, ensuring earlier estimates are replaced consistently.

### 🌐 English–Hindi Consistency
Review of English and Hindi email pairs to verify that timings, policy information, passenger instructions, and overall meaning remain consistent.

### ♻️ Reusable Prompt Template
Development and testing of a reusable email-generation prompt using a separate fictional flight scenario to assess its adaptability.

## ⚙️ 4. Project Workflow

<escape>1.</escape> **Establish the Facts** — Identify scheduled departure, expected departure, applicable policies, and missing information.

<escape>2.</escape> **Generate Drafts** — Use structured prompts to create passenger communications for different channels.

<escape>3.</escape> **Evaluate the Output** — Assess factual accuracy, completeness, clarity, tone, and passenger suitability.

<escape>4.</escape> **Verify and Correct** — Compare AI-generated statements with the supplied sources and remove unsupported claims.

<escape>5.</escape> **Update Communications** — Apply revised operational timings consistently across all relevant messages.

<escape>6.</escape> **Check Translation Consistency** — Compare English and Hindi communications for equivalent facts and instructions.

<escape>7.</escape> **Test Reusability** — Apply the final reusable prompt to a separate fictional flight scenario and review the result.

## 🛡️ 8. Responsible AI Use

This project follows a human-in-the-loop approach in which AI supports drafting, evaluation, and translation, while the contributor remains responsible for checking the facts and approving the final communication.

The process emphasizes:

- **Source-grounded responses:** Flight facts and policy claims must be supported by supplied information.
- **Transparency:** Unknown or unconfirmed details are explicitly flagged.
- **No fabricated assurances:** Estimated timings are never presented as guaranteed outcomes.
- **Human accountability:** Generated messages are reviewed and corrected before hypothetical use.
- **Consistency:** Revised timings and translated messages are checked against the same source information.

**Scope limitation:** This is an academic simulation using fictional flight information and policies. It is not connected to live airline systems, does not access real-time flight data, and does not send messages to actual passengers.

## 🏆 9. Project Outcome

The project establishes a structured, repeatable approach to AI-assisted aviation communication that integrates prompt design, factual verification, operational updates, multilingual review, and human oversight.

It demonstrates how generative AI can support airline service workflows while reducing the risk of unsupported claims, inconsistent updates, and misleading passenger information.
